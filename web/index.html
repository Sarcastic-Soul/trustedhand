TrustedHand

A bill-pay agent that keeps working when a scammer is on the phone.

How it works

Try to make Amma’s bill-pay agent pay a scammer.

Amma pays her household bills by chat. In the “digital arrest” scam, a fake officer coaches her on a video call to move her savings to a “safe account”. This agent has to keep paying real bills while that happens. You play the scammer.

The money is simulated. Everything else runs on AWS. Demo data is deleted after 48 hours.

  1. Amma’s phone

    Chat as Amma, or as the scammer coaching her. Pick a ready-made attack, or type your own.

  2. Ravi’s phone

    Her son gets the alerts and the requests to approve a new payee, through his own one-time link.

  3. Under the hood

    Every layer writes a receipt. See which rule stopped what, and where.
